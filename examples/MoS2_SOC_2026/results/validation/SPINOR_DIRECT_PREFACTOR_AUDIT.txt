Independent screened-direct spinor prefactor audit — 2026-10-23

Result: the proposed universal 1/nspinor=1/2 direct-kernel convention error is
not present in the audited native TDA matvec or BGW kernel export/reader path.
No source changes or GPU work were performed. This rules out that specific
normalization hypothesis; it does not explain the remaining spectrum difference.
Source reviewed: soc_tutorial_source f075e4e796f996719a1c9a48ce48d6abb5291424.

1. Spin contractions and scalar/spinor split
For spinors the direct operator is
  -1/Nk sum[k',s,t,mu,nu] conj(pc[k,c,t,mu]) pc[k',c',t,mu]
       W[k-k',mu,nu] pv[k,v,s,nu] conj(pv[k',v',s,nu]) X[c',v',k'].
The two spin sums are independent, not averaged. Native _encode_A (stack
matvec lines197–209), _decode_tm (288–296) and _decode_v (299–309) perform
exactly those sums. They contain no spinor-count denominator. The stored psi
spinor axis is unsharded, so MPI reductions do not divide its two components.

Native build_bse_stack_matvec (895–955) returns D+VX-WX. Its only spin weight
is exchange_spin_weight:1 for spinors,2 for a scalar singlet,0 scalar triplet
(bse_preconditioner.py:64–89). BGW intkernel.f90:224 uses
  factor=-8*pi/(Omega*Nk)*scaling
for all direct parts; the spinor branch244–247 leaves fac_d=factor. The
additional factor2 at1133/1138 belongs only to exchange and explicitly excludes
BSE_KERNEL_SPINOR. BGW mtxel_kernel sums both spinor components into each pair
matrix before w_sum; there is no spinor average there either. Both calculations
have nspin1,nspinor2 and 6*8*64=3072 transitions, not duplicated scalar sectors.

2. Nk, volume and Rydberg units
The native W term uses W_R=IFFT_ortho(W_q), then
  FFT_ortho(IFFT_ortho(T)*W_R)/sqrt(Nk).
Expanding the unitary transforms gives exactly sum[k'] W[k-k']T[k']/Nk.
The coarse-to-fine embedding includes sqrt(Nfine/Ncoarse), preserving this
normalization. The CUDA outer/fused factories (ffi/fft.py:2295,2415,2443)
pass the product of the inverse/forward unitary scales,1/Nk; CPP receives
that scalar and introduces no spin-dependent normalization. Their remaining
decode division is the same sqrt(Nk) as the XLA route. The0.5 transformations
elsewhere in the pair solver are non-TDA F/G coordinates and are not used here.

The exporter stores body/wing/exchange with Omega/(8*pi), while the BGW reader
uses -8*pi/(Omega*Nk) for direct and its opposite sign for exchange. Including
the head screening restoration, these compose to -C W C*/Nk and +C V C*/Nk.
No nspinor enters the exporter scaling (bsemat_algebra.py:183–208;
bsemat_export.py:713–730,836–842). Both raw files report identical cell volume
702.201201287347 bohr^3 and nspinor2. The native slab Coulomb has8*pi/q^2 in
Rydberg (vcoul/slab_2d.py:149), consistent with BGW's8*pi convention.

The1/nspinor factor DOES occur in optical epsilon prefactors after the solve,
in both native absorption_common.py and BGW absp.f90. It scales oscillator
response and cannot shift eigenvalues or create the observed binding ratio.

3. Independent raw-HDF amplitude evidence
Streamed Frobenius norms (native / original BGW):
  head      55.53538271 / 55.53975105   ratio0.99992135
  wing     151.12762567 /151.74994671   ratio0.99589904
  body     367.98834630 /371.51893635   ratio0.99049688
  exchange 515.49754931 /515.33260820   ratio1.00032007
All blocks have shape[9,9,12,12,12,12,2]. These gauge-invariant amplitudes
exclude a uniform exported direct scale of1/2 or2. Raw elementwise overlaps are
poor, especially exchange, so the files must NOT be described as gauge-aligned
or entrywise equivalent. The explicit-full WFN and original expanded-wedge
wavefunctions can carry different phases/degenerate gauges. KERNEL_NORMS.json
and kernel_norm_probe.py preserve the streamed measurement and its limitation.

4. Correct continuum for the binding comparison
Use each BSE's own fine-grid independent transition minimum. Native report:
3.23338eV gap,2.5110195eV lowest root ->0.72236eV dark binding (reported
precision). BGW eigenvalues_noeh.dat gives minimum3.1585562eV, so with lowest
root1.5915516eV its binding is1.5670046eV. The ratio remains about0.461, but a
binding ratio is a nonlinear eigenvalue result, not a measurement of a kernel
coefficient. Coarse GW gaps must not replace these fine-grid thresholds.

The parent's actual BGW-reader run on the native kernel is the appropriate
