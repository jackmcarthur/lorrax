## Standing doctrine a new agent must not violate

1. **Scaling target**: thousands of low-memory processes; no O(N_μ²) object
   may be *required* to fit on one rank in lim P→∞. Exactly **two plans per
   solve family** (local fast path + input-flag-gated distributed path);
   delete other variants. "Fastest at today's P" is not the design criterion.
2. **Square process meshes only** for the foreseeable future.
3. Never silent fallbacks: an explicit backend/tier request that can't be
   honored **refuses at resolve time with the reason**; `auto` may demote but
   must announce.
4. Communication/memory claims are proven from the **compiled HLO collective
   table**, not from Python intent (QUALITY_PATTERNS #4).
5. Env grants capability, never silently selects policy (#8); every
   env-default change carries its measurement and scope (#9).
6. Physics gates are bit-identity where the change is data-movement-only, and
   invariant-based (residuals, Kramers degeneracy, implied-Vxc) otherwise.
