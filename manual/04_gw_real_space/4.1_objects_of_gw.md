# 4.1 The objects of GW in real space

> **Reader note.** This chapter writes every quantity on the real-space grid,
> $K(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}')$. In the code, $\mathbf{r}$ and $\mathbf{r}'$ run over
> the far smaller set of interpolation points $\{\mathbf{r}_\mu\}$ of Chapter 5;
> nothing below changes under that substitution.

GW replaces $V_{xc}$ with the self-energy $\Sigma = iGW$. Every ingredient is a
two-point kernel: $G(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';t)$, $\chi(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';t)$,
$W(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';\omega)$, $\Sigma(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';\omega)$. The
one-shot loop:

1. Green's functions from the DFT bands. The formalism needs only the
   occupied-only and empty-only propagators

   $$
   G_o(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';\tau) = \sum_{v}^{occ} \psi_v(\mathbf{r})\,\psi_v^*(\mathbf{r}')\,
   e^{\varepsilon_v \tau},
   \qquad
   G_e(\mathbf{r},\mathbf{r}';\tau) = \sum_{c}^{emp} \psi_c(\mathbf{r})\,\psi_c^*(\mathbf{r}')\,
   e^{-\varepsilon_c \tau},
   $$

   energies measured from the chemical potential so both decay with $\tau$. For
   spinors each $\psi\psi^*$ is a $2\times2$ spin matrix; products of two
   propagators carry an implied spin trace (§5.2). Each is a sum over one occupancy
   class only (§4.2).

2. Irreducible polarizability $\chi^0 = -iGG$: in the time domain, a pointwise
   product of the two propagators.

3. Screened interaction from $W = v + v\chi^0 W$, solved as a linear system in the
   compressed basis at the few frequencies Σ needs (Ch. 7). The kernel $v$ and its
   truncation: Chapter 6.

4. Self-energy $\Sigma = iGW$: bare exchange $\Sigma_x = -G_o v$ plus correlation
   $\Sigma_c(\omega)$ built from $W - v$, so bare exchange is counted once. Static
   COHSEX collapses $\Sigma_c$ to a screened-exchange-minus-bare-exchange term plus
   a Coulomb-hole term at $\omega = 0$; GN-PPM restores the frequency dependence
   through a plasmon-pole representation of $W$ (§7.4–7.5). BerkeleyGW's printed
   SX/CH decomposition groups these differently; only totals are cross-comparable
   (App. E).

5. Band projection $\Sigma_{ij\mathbf{k}}(\omega) =
   \langle \psi_{i\mathbf{k}}|\Sigma(\omega)|\psi_{j\mathbf{k}}\rangle$ and the
   quasiparticle equation $\varepsilon^{QP} = \varepsilon^{DFT} +
   \langle \Sigma(\varepsilon^{QP}) - V_{xc} \rangle$, solved per §4.5.
