# 4.5 From Σ to quasiparticle energies

$$
\varepsilon^{QP}_{n\mathbf{k}} \;=\; \varepsilon^{DFT}_{n\mathbf{k}}
+ \big\langle n\mathbf{k} \big|\, \Sigma(\varepsilon^{QP}_{n\mathbf{k}}) - V_{xc} \,\big| n\mathbf{k} \big\rangle .
$$

`qp_solver` selects the treatment:

- `one_shot_dft` (default): evaluate at the DFT energy and linearize,
  $\varepsilon^{QP} = \varepsilon^{DFT} + Z\langle\Sigma(\varepsilon^{DFT}) -
  V_{xc}\rangle$, $Z = [1 - \partial\langle\Sigma\rangle/\partial\omega]^{-1}$.
  Both `eqp0` (zeroth-order) and `eqp1` (linearized) are written, matching
  BerkeleyGW conventions.
- `fixed_point`: on-shell solve $\varepsilon = \varepsilon^{DFT} +
  \langle\Sigma(\varepsilon) - V_{xc}\rangle$ per state on the sampled grid.
  Dynamic modes only.
- `self_consistent`: Σ rebuilt each iteration, QSGW-style, from wavefunctions
  rotated by the previous iteration's quasiparticle unitary, to `sc_tol_ev`.
  Quadrature shapes are fixed across iterations, so iterations after the first hit
  the compilation cache; the `eqp` columns keep the at-$E_{DFT}$ formula in all
  three modes.

The fixed point is accelerated by rCROP (`sc_accelerator = "rcrop"`), a
residual-history method in the CROP–Anderson family [Wan & Międlar, *On the
Convergence of the CROP-Anderson Acceleration Method*], stable where Pulay-type
mixing is not for complex-valued Σ; `linear` with `sc_mixing` is the fallback, and
`sc_history_depth` (default 5) sets the history.

Unless `no_degen_averaging` is set, Σ diagonals are averaged over degenerate
multiplets (`degen_avg_tol_ry`) before the QP solve, as in BerkeleyGW.

> **BGW correspondence.** `eqp0`/`eqp1` match `sigma.log`/`eqp.dat` semantics;
> static modes have $Z = 1$ so the two coincide. The Z-factor derivative is a
> central difference on the sampled ω grid with 0.5 eV spacing (the BerkeleyGW
> `finite_difference_spacing` default), at no extra Σ cost and not
> user-configurable.
