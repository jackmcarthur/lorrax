# 8.2 Four-density ISDF

Each Lorentz channel gets its own ISDF fit: per $\mu_L \in \{0,1,2,3\}$,

$$
\rho^{\mu_L}_{mn\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf{q};\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{ab}
\psi^*_{m,\mathbf{k}-\mathbf{q},a}(\mathbf{r})\,\tilde\gamma^{\mu_L}_{ab}\,
\psi_{n,\mathbf{k},b}(\mathbf{r})
\;\approx\; \sum_\lambda \zeta^{\mu_L}_{q,\lambda}(\mathbf{r})\,
\rho^{\mu_L}_{mn\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf{q};\mathbf{r}_\lambda),
$$

the $\mu_L = 0$ fit being exactly Chapter 5. The three current densities peak in
different spatial regions than the charge, so a bispinor run reads two centroid
files: the scalar set for $\mu_L = 0$ and a second set generated with the current
density as the k-means weight (`kmeans_cli --density-mode current`, from the
Gordon-decomposed Pauli current of the occupied manifold). The counts are
independent; channel-aware centroids improve transverse reconstruction error by
~13–15% on MoS₂ at matched cost, and the recommended production ratio is roughly
3:1 charge:current points, since the transverse fits are the expensive stage.

The normal-equations structure of §5.3 carries over per channel with one
difference: the transverse fitting matrices are Hermitian but **indefinite** (the
spin-pair kernel $\tilde\gamma^{i*}\otimes\tilde\gamma^i$ has eigenvalues $\pm1$),
so the $\mu_L = i$ channels are factorized by an LU-family solve while the charge
channel keeps Cholesky (`factor_c_q`, dispatched per channel). Each channel
produces its own $V^{\mu_L}_{q,\mu\nu}$ via §5.3's reciprocal-space contraction,
the transverse ones carrying the projector $\delta_{ij} - K_iK_j/|K|^2$ from
§8.1's propagator.

Cost relative to a scalar run of the same system: four ζ fits instead of one
(about twice the total ISDF rank at the 3:1 ratio), 4-component wavefunction
storage, and the nine $(i,j)$ tile contractions of $\Sigma^B$. The empirical
band-count ceilings on fixed hardware are roughly 3× lower than scalar
(pair-density workspace, not $V_q$, is the binding peak); the planner (§12.2)
accounts for this.
