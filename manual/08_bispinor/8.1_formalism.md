# 8.1 Bispinor GW with the Breit interaction

Beyond scalar-relativistic + SOC treatments, the current–current (transverse
photon) part of the electron–electron interaction contributes exchange terms that
grow with nuclear charge; LORRAX computes them from four-component wavefunctions.
Two-component QE spinors are lifted to Dirac bispinors by kinetic balance,

$$
\Psi_{n\mathbf{k}}(\mathbf{G}) = \begin{pmatrix}\psi_L\\\psi_S\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\psi_S = \tfrac{\alpha_{\rm FS}}{2}\,\big[\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot(\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{G})\big]\,\psi_L,
$$

and all densities are written $\rho^{\mu_L} = \Psi^\dagger\tilde\gamma^{\mu_L}\Psi$
with $\tilde\gamma^{\mu_L} \equiv \gamma^0\gamma^{\mu_L}$ ($\tilde\gamma^0 = I_4$,
$\tilde\gamma^i = \alpha^i$). In Coulomb gauge the bare photon propagator is
block-diagonal,

$$
D^{\mu_L\nu_L}(\mathbf{K}) = \begin{pmatrix} 4\pi/|\mathbf{K}|^2 & 0 \\
0 & (4\pi/|\mathbf{K}|^2)\,(\delta_{ij}-K_iK_j/|\mathbf{K}|^2) \end{pmatrix},
\qquad \mathbf{K}=\mathbf{q}+\mathbf{G},
$$

so the self-energy splits exactly into a charge channel and a transverse (Breit)
channel:

$$
\Sigma^C(12) = -\,G(12)\,W_{00}(12), \qquad
\Sigma^B_{\alpha\beta}(12) = -\sum_{i,j=1}^{3} \tilde\gamma^i_{\alpha\gamma}\,
G_{\gamma\delta}(12)\,\tilde\gamma^j_{\delta\beta}\,D^{ij}(12).
$$

$\Sigma^C$ is the ordinary GW self-energy evaluated on bispinors: the charge
polarizability $\chi^0_{00} = -\mathrm{Tr}[G(12)G(21)]$ reuses the scalar minimax
machinery of Chapter 7 with the spin axis grown from 2 to 4, and the full static
COHSEX / GN-PPM screening applies. In the default mode $\Sigma^B$ is the
bare-Breit (Dirac–Hartree–Fock-like) exchange, with $D^{ij}$ the *unscreened*
transverse propagator; a screened $W^{ij}$ exists, but only as a static
COHSEX-only packed mode (§8.4). $\Sigma^B$ vanishes as
$\alpha_{\rm FS}\to 0$, since $\psi_S \to 0$ removes the blocks that $\alpha^i$
couples, recovering the two-component code exactly.

The $\mathbf K = 0$ slot of every block needs its own treatment, and the four
channels do not share one: the physics owner for all of it is
`docs/theory/four-current-head-corrections.md`, and Appendix B is the reader's
route in. How the layer is wired into the rest of the code — every producer,
object, shape and sharding — is `docs/architecture/four_current_wiring.md`.
