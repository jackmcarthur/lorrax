# 8.3 Assembling Σ = Σᶜ + Σᴮ

The charge channel runs through the machinery of Chapters 5–7 unchanged: bispinor
$\chi^0_{00}$, Dyson solve for $W_{00}$, static COHSEX or GN-PPM $\Sigma^C(\omega)$,
all on 4-component wavefunction values at the scalar centroid set. The transverse
term contracts the nine $(i,j)$ tiles of the bare propagator against the Green's
function between the current-set centroids,

$$
\Sigma^B_{\alpha\beta} = -\sum_{i,j}\tilde\gamma^i_{\alpha\gamma}\,
G_{\gamma\delta}(\mathbf{r}_\mu,\mathbf{r}_\nu)\,\tilde\gamma^j_{\delta\beta}\,
V^{TT,ij}_{q,\mu\nu},
$$

with the γ-matrix contraction strategy selectable (`gamma_contract_mode =
take | einsum | scan`, a performance knob with identical results), and is added to
the bare charge exchange before band projection.

Symmetry treatment differs by channel, and the difference is in the $q$ list, not
in the unfold. The $V_q$ orchestrator resolves an orbit-closed IBZ $q$ list per
centroid set (charge and transverse are separate facts and get separate lines in
`gwjax.out`); a set that is not orbit-closed falls back to the full Brillouin
zone with an announcement, and `LORRAX_FORCE_FULL_BZ=1` forces that fallback for
both. Four-component *wavefunction* rotation still does not exist —
`docs/architecture/symmetry_register.md` owns that status and the upgrade path.
When validating, compare the physical, band-projected $\Sigma^B$ between runs, not
intermediate per-channel $V_q$ tiles, whose magnitudes are gauge-dependent.

The screened four-current alternative to this section's bare contraction is the
packed sixteen-block $\Sigma$ of §8.4: same Green function, same band
projection, with $D^{IJ}$ replaced by a Dyson-solved $W^{IJ}$.
