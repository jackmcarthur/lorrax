# 6.4 q → 0

At $\mathbf{q}\to0$ the bare kernel diverges as $1/q^2$ while the head of the
polarizability vanishes as $q^2$ (gauge invariance; equivalently the f-sum rule),
with coefficient set by the transition dipoles. The head of $W$ is therefore finite
but not computable from the discrete grid alone: LORRAX builds the $q^2$
coefficient from dipole matrix elements (`psp.get_dipole_mtxels`, §9.1) via the
s-tensor machinery, or takes overrides (`wcoul0_source`, `whead_0freq`,
`whead_imfreq`, including the imaginary-frequency head the GN-PPM fit needs).

Appendix B.
