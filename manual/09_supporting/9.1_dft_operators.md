# 9.1 DFT operator infrastructure

LORRAX reconstructs the pieces of the Kohn–Sham Hamiltonian it needs from `WFN.h5`
plus the pseudopotentials, rather than reading them all from QE. The kinetic and
ionic (local + nonlocal) parts are exported once per system
(`python -m gw.kin_ion_io` → `kin_ion.h5`); the Hartree potential follows from the
density; $V_{xc}$ is rebuilt through the same functional evaluation as the DFT
(`psp.dft_operators`). The reconstructed Hamiltonian reproduces QE eigenvalues to
sub-meV for nonmagnetic systems. The standalone $V_{xc}$ reconstruction is
currently spin-unpolarized (no $B_{xc}$), which limits the rebuilt-Hamiltonian
path, not the GW pipeline, for magnetic systems.

Dipole matrix elements are computed at the DFT level from the canonical velocity
operator $\hat{\mathbf v} = \mathbf p + \mathbf v_{NL}$
(`python -m psp.get_dipole_mtxels` → `dipole.h5`); they feed the $q\to0$ head of
$W$ (App. B) and the BSE absorption spectra (§9.2). Adding the commutator
$i[\mathbf r, \Sigma^{GW}]$ upgrades them to GW-level oscillator strengths.
BerkeleyGW's convention is $\mathbf p - \mathbf v_{NL}$; the sign difference is
invisible on the diagonal and matters off-diagonal (App. E).

Also in this layer: Sternheimer linear-response solves (preconditioned iterative
solvers over the occupied-projected subspace), Chebyshev/KPM density-of-states
tools, and pseudoband compression of the conduction manifold, used by the
band-structure interpolation of §9.3 and available as standalone utilities under
`src/psp/` and `src/solvers/`.
