# 9.2 Bethe–Salpeter absorption

The BSE Hamiltonian is never formed. In the ISDF basis its action on an
electron–hole vector is a matrix–vector product built from band sums and centroid
contractions obeying the two-sums rule: the direct term contracts the vector with
$W_{\mu\nu}$ from the GW restart (the screened interaction is already in the
$(\mu,\nu)$ basis), the exchange term with $V_{q,\mu\nu}$, at
$O(n_{vc}\,n_\mu + n_\mu^2)$ per application instead of the $O(n_{vc}^2)$ of a
stored kernel. Everything downstream is iterative linear algebra on that matvec,
sharded over the same device mesh as the GW step.

The solver menu (`python -m bse.bse_jax`, argparse CLI rather than `cohsex.in`):

| Solver | Flag | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Haydock recursion | `--rpa` / `--bse` | absorption spectra, cheapest |
| Lanczos, full reorthogonalization | `--lanczos --n-reorth -1` | exciton eigenpairs (spinor BSE needs full reorth) |
| Davidson | (see `davidson_absorption.py`) | lowest excitons, preconditioned |
| FEAST contour windows | `--feast-*` | interior eigenvalues in an energy window |
| KPM | `--kpm-*` | excitonic DOS |
| Shifted GMRES | `--gmres-*` | exact $W_c(\omega)$ response at chosen frequencies |

with `--tda` versus full (non-TDA) matrices, `--n-val/--n-cond` band windows, and
`--px/--py` the processor grid. Spectra come out as $\varepsilon_2(\omega)$ with
Lorentzian broadening and Kramers–Kronig $\varepsilon_1$, in BerkeleyGW-format
`.dat` files; eigenvectors in `eigenvectors.h5`-compatible layout.

Comparing against BerkeleyGW absorption requires matching six conventions (dipole
operator sign, eqp source, head injection, SOC band counting, occupation
resolution, broadening/iteration count); the checklist and a validated Si Haydock
comparison are in Appendix E.
