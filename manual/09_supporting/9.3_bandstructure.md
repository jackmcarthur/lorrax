# 9.3 Quasiparticle band structures

Quasiparticle corrections are computed on the uniform GW grid; band structures
along arbitrary k-paths come from a Hamiltonian transformation
(`python -m bandstructure.htransform`): the kinetic + ionic operator (from
`kin_ion.h5`) plus the updated Hartree and Σ contributions are assembled in the
centroid basis and diagonalized at any requested k, so the interpolation carries
the full nonlocality of Σ rather than Wannier-style smoothness assumptions.
K-paths are given in the `K_POINTS {crystal_b}` block of the input (QE
band-structure syntax); `--eqp-file` selects which quasiparticle energies enter,
and `--plot` renders directly.

The diagonalizations are dense complex128 at $n_\mu \times n_\mu$: beyond a few
thousand centroids a single matrix is tens of GB, and the distributed
linear-algebra backend (§2.3, §12.3) is required rather than optional. Rotated
quasiparticle wavefunctions for downstream consumers are written by the GW step
itself (`qp_rotations.h5`, `postprocess.rotate_wfn_to_qp`).
