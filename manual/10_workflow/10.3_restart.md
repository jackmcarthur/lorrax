# 10.3 Restart semantics

`restart = true` (the default) reuses every on-disk stage whose inputs are
unchanged: the ζ store and ISDF tensors are keyed to the centroid set, band
window, and cutoffs, so a rerun with a new `compute_mode`, ω grid, or QP solver
skips straight past the fit and $V_q$ (this is how §3.2 upgrades §3.1 for
seconds). Any change to the wavefunctions, centroids, band window, or cutoffs
invalidates the store; the code detects the mismatch from the recorded metadata
and refits rather than reusing silently. Restarted and fresh runs are
bit-identical, a frozen invariance gate of the test suite (§13.5). Delete the
store (or point `restart = false`) to force a clean rebuild; nothing else is
stateful between runs.
