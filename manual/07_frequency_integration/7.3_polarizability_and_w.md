# 7.3 χ and the Dyson solve

The pipeline needs χ at two kinds of frequency: static, and one imaginary probe for
the plasmon-pole fit (§7.4). χ is never formed on a real-frequency grid.

Static: a noncrossing GL rule on the transition interval
$[\text{min gap}, \text{max transition}]$, then

$$
\chi^0(\mathbf{r}_\mu,\mathbf{r}_\nu; 0) \;=\; -\sum_l \alpha_l\;
G_o(\mathbf{r}_\mu,\mathbf{r}_\nu; \tau_l)\, G_e^*(\mathbf{r}_\mu,\mathbf{r}_\nu; \tau_l),
$$

assembled per lattice vector and FFT'd to all $\mathbf{q}$ at once (§4.3). At
$i\omega_p$ only the target changes: the resonant + antiresonant pair sums to
$2x/(x^2+\omega_p^2)$, fitted by the imaginary-axis rule of §7.2 on the same
interval and contracted identically. Imaginary frequencies keep the integrand in
the decaying regime, which is why the probe sits on the imaginary axis.

The screened interaction then solves, per $\mathbf{q}$ and frequency,

$$
\big(I - V_q\, \kappa\,\chi^0_q\big)\, W_q \;=\; V_q,
\qquad
\kappa = \frac{2}{\sqrt{N_k}\; n_{\text{spin}}\, n_{\text{spinor}}},
$$

an $n_\mu \times n_\mu$ dense system (LU in the default backend); the self-energy
consumes $W^c = W - v$. κ carries the lattice-FT and spin normalization and is the
usual source of factor-of-two mismatches against other χ conventions.
`screening_solver = jax_native` (default) is the direct sharded solve;
`cublasmp_ffi` is a symmetrized low-memory form ($v = XX^\dagger$,
$W = X(I - X^\dagger \kappa\chi X)^{-1}X^\dagger$) fused into the distributed
cuBLASMp path (§2.3). The $\mathbf{q}\to0$ head is fitted separately from dipole
data and injected analytically (App. B).
