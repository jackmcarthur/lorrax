# 7.5 Assembling Σᶜ(ω)

With $G$'s poles at band energies and $W$'s fitted poles at
$\pm\Omega_p$, every causal term has a denominator

$$
d(\omega)=\omega-\varepsilon_n\mp\Omega_p+i\eta,
$$

where the configured $\eta$ is applied exactly once. The band and pole
phases remain separable, so one node uses the established
`build_G_tau -> lattice FFT -> G(t)W(t) -> band projection` executor. No
state-by-pole carrier is formed.

The shared MPA/GN/HL planner partitions the Cartesian
$(\varepsilon_n,\Omega_p,\omega)$ support into uniform denominator boxes.
Each box is certified directly against $1/d$ at fixed $\eta$, including
boxes whose real part crosses zero. This replaces the retired HGL target and
its PPM-only core/stripe/slab adapter; there is one planner and one executor,
not a fallback route.

Band projection produces the single complex carrier

$$
X_k=\psi_k^\dagger\sigma_k^\tau\psi_k.
$$

Scalar time/frequency coefficients are applied around that carrier. The
anti-Hermitian completion required by a causal branch is performed once after
the time sum. This preserves all-(P) sharding of the large spatial objects
and avoids the retired split-real projection path.

**sigma_quadrature_eps** owns the per-box target and
**sigma_quadrature_reduction_seconds** owns the bounded rule-reduction budget.
The physical cost is the sum of node counts over resident window batches;
output-frequency points are a comparatively cheap coefficient fold and are
not physical time dispatches.
