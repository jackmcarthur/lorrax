# 7.5 Assembling Σᶜ(ω)

With $G$'s poles at the band energies and $W$'s at $\pm\Omega_{\mu\nu}$,

$$
\Sigma^c_{\mu\nu}(\omega) \;\sim\; \sum_{n\in occ}
\frac{(\psi\psi^*)_n\, B_{\mu\nu}}{\omega - \varepsilon_n + \Omega_{\mu\nu} - i\eta}
\;+\; \sum_{n\in emp}
\frac{(\psi\psi^*)_n\, B_{\mu\nu}}{\omega - \varepsilon_n - \Omega_{\mu\nu} + i\eta}.
$$

Each denominator has the shape $\omega - S$ with composite energy
$S = \varepsilon_n \pm \Omega$, and $e^{-S\tau} = e^{-\varepsilon_n\tau}
e^{\mp\Omega\tau}$ factorizes: at each node the band sum and the pole sum proceed
independently, contracted through the same lattice-FFT machinery as χ. The per-node
kernel is $G(i\tau_l) \times W(\tau_l)$ with $W_{\mu\nu}(\tau) = B_{\mu\nu}
e^{-i\Omega_{\mu\nu}\tau}$, projected onto the ω grid by $e^{\pm i\omega\tau_l}$.

The ω grid (`sigma_omega_min_ev`…`max`…`step`, from the Fermi reference) splits
into halves, each pairing with the occupied and empty sums: four branches, with
exactly one crossing branch per half ($\omega > 0$: the empty denominators
$\omega - \varepsilon_c - \Omega$ cross; $\omega < 0$: the occupied ones). The
sign-definite branch of each half takes a single noncrossing GL rule; the crossing
branch is split by the threshold $T = \omega_{max} + 1.5\,\xi$, with $\xi$ the
broadening (`sigma_regularization_ev`, default 0.25 eV):

| Window | Region | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| core | $\varepsilon \le T$, $\Omega \le T$: poles can reach grid frequencies | crossing HGL, bandwidth $A = 2T/\xi$ |
| a-stripe | $\varepsilon > T$, $\Omega \le T$ | noncrossing GL, shifted range |
| b-slab | $\Omega > T$, any $\varepsilon$ | noncrossing GL, shifted range |

Only the core pays the crossing quadrature; the stripe and slab, holding most
$(n,\mu\nu)$ combinations, ride exponential rules. Invalid-pole elements are
removed from all windows and handled by the static-limit term (§7.4).

Band projection contracts the kernel with wavefunction values at the centroids;
whether the real and imaginary channels of the kernel must be projected
separately is decided by the window's consumer, not by the projection (below).
`sigma_omega_accumulation = kij | kij_stream` trades memory
against recomputation, equivalent to ~1e-13; `auto` picks by available memory.
$\Sigma_x$ is frequency-independent, assembled from $G_o$ and $V_q$ (§5.3), and
added at the end; the $q\to0$ head enters analytically from the fitted head pole
(App. B).

## Channel structure of the projection

The per-node operand splits into real channels, $\sigma^\tau = \sigma_R +
i\,\sigma_I$ with $\sigma_{R/I}$ real, and the band projection acts on each:

$$
S_R(k) = \psi_k^\dagger\,\sigma_R\,\psi_k,\qquad
S_I(k) = \psi_k^\dagger\,\sigma_I\,\psi_k,\qquad
X_k \equiv \psi_k^\dagger\,\sigma^\tau_k\,\psi_k = S_R + i\,S_I,
$$

the last identity by bilinearity of the projection in $\sigma$. Which channels a
window needs is a property of its ω-space consumer. The Laplace windows (single,
a-stripe, b-slab) accumulate the full complex product with the coefficient
$c = \mathrm{pref}\cdot\alpha_{\mathrm{eff}}\cdot e^{i\,\mathrm{sign}\,\omega\tau_l}$:

$$
\mathrm{contrib}(\omega) \;=\; c\,(S_R + i\,S_I) \;=\; c\,X ,
$$

so one complex GEMM chain per node suffices, and LORRAX computes $X$ directly
for these windows — the default and only Laplace projection path — halving
their projection GEMM work, reduce-scatter payload, and device-to-host volume;
the identity uses no symmetry, so it survives HL probes and any hermiticity
defect of the fitted $B_q$.

The crossing core window cannot be merged. Its HGL quadrature keeps
$\mathrm{Im}[c\,\sigma]$ elementwise before projection, which projects to

$$
\mathrm{contrib}(\omega) \;=\; \mathrm{Re}(c)\,S_I + \mathrm{Im}(c)\,S_R ,
$$

two independent real ω-weight vectors: both channels are consumed. Nor are they
recoverable from $X$ alone. On a Laplace slice both factors of the kernel carry
real decaying phases, so $\sigma^\tau$ is Hermitian (the Hadamard product of
Hermitian matrices is Hermitian), $S_R$ and $i\,S_I$ are then both Hermitian,
and the Toeplitz decomposition of $X$ into Hermitian plus anti-Hermitian parts
returns $(X,\,0)$ rather than $(S_R,\,i\,S_I)$: splitting a Hermitian $X$ within
one real subspace is non-unique, $n^2$ real degrees of freedom in $X$ against
$2n^2$ in the pair. On a crossing slice the counting is worse ($2n^2$ against
$4n^2$) and no per-slice symmetry exists at all: both factors carry unimodular
phases with symmetric exponents, $\sigma^\tau$ is neither Hermitian nor
complex-symmetric, and the only surviving relation,
$[\sigma^\tau]^\dagger = \sigma^{-\tau}$, pairs abscissae the one-sided HGL grid
never provides. Full derivation, the merge-legality gate $S_R + i S_I = X$
(enforced at $10^{-12}$ through the production kernels), and the crossing
falsification residual: the channel hermiticity memo
(wk_REL/DERIVATION_channel_hermiticity.md, 2026-07-28).
