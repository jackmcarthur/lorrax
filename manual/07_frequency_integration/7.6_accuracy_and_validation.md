# 7.6 Accuracy: knobs, expectations, validation

The frequency machinery introduces exactly four approximations beyond the ISDF basis,
each with one input key and one clean convergence behavior. In practice their errors
are ordered:

1. **The plasmon-pole model** (§7.4) — the physical approximation, and the largest.
   One pole per matrix element reproduces quasiparticle energies of gapped systems
   at the level plasmon-pole GW is known for (tens of meV vs. full-frequency);
   satellite structure and metallic screening are outside its design envelope. The
   knob is categorical, not continuous: `compute_mode = static_cohsex | gn_ppm`
   today, multipole extension on the roadmap. The *diagnostic* is the invalid-pole
   census in the log — a rising invalid fraction is the model warning you.
2. **The broadening** ξ (`sigma_regularization_ev`, 0.25 eV default) — a physical
   linewidth parameter, not a numerical tolerance: it smooths Σ(ω) on its own scale
   and sets the crossing-window cost ($A = 2T/\xi$, §7.5). Halving ξ sharpens
   spectral structure and roughly doubles core-window nodes. Converge it the way you
   would a smearing width.
3. **The ω-grid** (`sigma_omega_*`) — pure sampling of a smooth-on-the-ξ-scale
   function; the 0.25–0.5 eV default steps resolve everything the broadening leaves.
4. **Minimax quadrature error** (`minimax_target_error`, $10^{-6}$ default) —
   deliberately negligible: μeV-scale against the meV-scale items above, at
   logarithmic cost (§7.2). There is no reason to loosen it.

The probe frequency `ppm_omega_p` deserves its own sentence: Godby–Needs fits are
famously flat in $\omega_p$ across the plasmonic range (roughly 1–4 Ry for ordinary
densities), and a two-point sweep is a cheap sanity check on any new material class.

**What the test suite pins permanently.** The frozen regression gates (§13.5) hold
end-to-end eqp outputs for static COHSEX vs BGW (Si), GN-PPM (MoS₂ fixture), and
bispinor GN-PPM to bit-level tolerances against golden files, plus invariance gates
(restart ≡ fresh, IBZ ≡ full-BZ, accumulation-mode equivalence at ~1e-13) that
protect exactly the machinery this chapter described. A passing suite on your
install means this chapter's pipeline reproduces the reference numbers on your
hardware.

> `bare_coulomb_cutoff` (§6.3), matched probe/`invalid_gpp_mode`, BGW broadening
> set to its minimum (LORRAX's ξ enters differently), and comparison of *total*
> Σ_c only — never branch-by-branch (§7.4). The full recipe is Appendix E.
