# 7.6 Accuracy and validation

Beyond the ISDF basis, the frequency machinery introduces four approximations, in
decreasing order of error: 1.) the plasmon-pole model itself, tens of meV against
full frequency for gapped systems, with the invalid-pole census as its diagnostic
and a categorical knob (`compute_mode`); 2.) the broadening ξ
(`sigma_regularization_ev`, 0.25 eV default), a physical linewidth that smooths
Σ(ω) on its own scale and sets the crossing-window cost ($A = 2T/\xi$), converged
like a smearing width; 3.) the ω grid (`sigma_omega_*`), plain sampling of a
function smooth on the ξ scale; and 4.) minimax quadrature error
(`minimax_target_error`, $10^{-6}$), $\mu$eV-scale at logarithmic cost, never worth
loosening. Godby–Needs fits are flat in `ppm_omega_p` across 1–4 Ry; a two-point
sweep is a cheap check on a new material class.
