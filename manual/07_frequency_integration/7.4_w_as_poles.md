# 7.4 W as poles: the Godby–Needs construction

The exact $W^c(\omega)$ is a sum of poles, one per charge-neutral excitation (the
excitations the BSE resolves, §9.2); a plasmon-pole model compresses this to one
pole pair per ISDF matrix element,

$$
W^c_{\mu\nu}(\omega) \;=\; \frac{2B_{\mu\nu}\,\Omega_{\mu\nu}}{\omega^2 - \Omega_{\mu\nu}^2}
\;=\; B_{\mu\nu}\left[\frac{1}{\omega-\Omega_{\mu\nu}} - \frac{1}{\omega+\Omega_{\mu\nu}}\right],
\qquad W^c(0) = -\frac{2B}{\Omega}.
$$

Two samples fix the two parameters: the static value and one imaginary-frequency
probe $z = i\omega_p$ (`ppm_omega_p`, default 2 Ry),

$$
\Omega^2_{\mu\nu} = \frac{-z^2\, W^c_{\mu\nu}(z)}{W^c_{\mu\nu}(0) - W^c_{\mu\nu}(z)},
\qquad
B_{\mu\nu} = -\tfrac{1}{2}\, W^c_{\mu\nu}(0)\,\Omega_{\mu\nu},
$$

with $\Omega = \sqrt{\mathrm{Re}\,\Omega^2}$. Sampling on the imaginary axis, where
$W^c$ is smooth, conditions the fit far better than real-axis sampling [Godby &
Needs, PRL **62**, 1169 (1989)]; results are insensitive to $\omega_p$ across the
plasmonic range.

A pole pair is valid when $\mathrm{Re}\,\Omega^2 > 0$; elements that fail
(typically small oscillatory off-diagonal entries) are counted in the log's fit
census and handled per `ppm_invalid_mode`: `static_limit` (default; BGW
`invalid_gpp_mode = 3`) replaces the pole by its exact $\Omega \to \infty$ limit,
an ω-independent static-COHSEX term $-\langle G_o W^c(0)\rangle +
\tfrac12\langle G_{RI} W^c(0)\rangle$ on the invalid elements; `zero` (BGW 0) drops
them; `2ry` (BGW 2) keeps live poles at `ppm_fallback_omega`.

The Hybertsen–Louie GPP, which fixes its pole from the static dielectric matrix and
the f-sum rule, is retained for comparison (`ppm_model = "hl"`, real probe above
all transitions); references in App. G. A multipole extension, several poles fitted
at several imaginary frequencies, reuses this machinery with a pole index and is on
the roadmap.
