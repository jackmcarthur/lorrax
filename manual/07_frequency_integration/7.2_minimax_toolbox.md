# 7.2 The minimax toolbox

All frequency integration reduces to three approximation problems, each solved by a
small optimized quadrature (Kim, Martyna & Ismail-Beigi, PRB **101**, 035139
(2020); shipped tables in App. C).

Noncrossing (GL), for $x \in [x_{min}, x_{max}]$, $x > 0$:

$$
\frac{1}{x} \approx \sum_{l=1}^{N} w_l\, e^{-t_l x},
$$

nodes and weights fitted to equioscillate the worst-case error, with $N$ growing
logarithmically in $R = x_{max}/x_{min}$. The shipped solver obeys
$\varepsilon \approx 0.31\, e^{-N(3.55/\ln R + 0.68)}$: at $R = 10^3$,
$\mu$-level accuracy near $N \approx 15$. Used for χ transitions and the Laplace
parts of Σ.

Crossing (HGL), for $x_{min} \le |x| \le x_{max}$ with sign changes:

$$
\frac{1}{x} \approx \sum_{l=1}^{N} w_l\, \sin(x\,\tau_l),
$$

odd by construction, $N$ roughly linear in $A = x_{max}/x_{min}$
($\varepsilon \approx e^{-0.93 - 14.25 N/A}$). The floor $x_{min}$ is the
broadening of §4.4, so crossing rules are the expensive ones; the windowing exists
mainly for their benefit.

Imaginary-frequency sampling: $x/(x^2 + \omega^2) \approx \sum_l \alpha_l
e^{-\tau_l x}$, which evaluates $\chi(i\omega_p)$ from the same time-domain
propagators (§7.3).

Denominators are partitioned into windows of bounded ratio before fitting (§4.4);
the Σ window layout is §7.5. Production runs draw rules from shipped tables
(`src/common/minimax_assets/`, $R$ to $10^5$, error bounds to $2\times10^{-7}$)
under the rule: smallest tabulated range ≥ requested, loosest tabulated error ≤
`minimax_target_error`, node count ≤ `minimax_max_nodes`; anything uncovered falls
back to the exact solver (`regenerate_minimax_tables` forces it). Error conventions
in App. C. `minimax_target_error` is the single accuracy dial, with logarithmic
cost in the noncrossing sector and linear in the crossing sector; the defaults
($10^{-6}$, 64-node cap) keep quadrature error negligible against the physical
approximations (§5.5, §7.4).
