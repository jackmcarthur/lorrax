# 5.3 The fit

Minimizing the squared interpolation error over all band pairs and k-points in the
window yields, per momentum transfer, the normal equations

$$
\boxed{\; C_q\, \zeta_q = Z_q \;}
\qquad
\sum_{\mu} C_{q,\nu\mu}\, \zeta_{q,\mu}(\mathbf{r}) = Z_{q,\nu}(\mathbf{r}),
$$

whose coefficients collapse (derivation standard; references in App. G) into
products of quasi-density matrices at the interpolation points,

$$
P_{\mathbf{k},ss'}(\mathbf{r}_\nu, \mathbf{x}) \;=\; \sum_{n \in \text{window}}
\psi^*_{n\mathbf{k},s}(\mathbf{r}_\nu)\; \psi_{n\mathbf{k},s'}(\mathbf{x}),
$$

$$
C_{q,\nu\mu} = \sum_{\mathbf{k}}\sum_{ss'} P^*_{\mathbf{k}-\mathbf{q},s's}(\mathbf{r}_\nu,\mathbf{r}_\mu)\,
P_{\mathbf{k},ss'}(\mathbf{r}_\nu,\mathbf{r}_\mu),
\qquad
Z_{q,\nu}(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{\mathbf{k}}\sum_{ss'} P^*_{\mathbf{k}-\mathbf{q},s's}(\mathbf{r}_\nu,\mathbf{r})\,
P_{\mathbf{k},ss'}(\mathbf{r}_\nu,\mathbf{r}).
$$

The $P_\mathbf{k}$ are single band sums (§4.2); all pair information enters through
their products. The k-sums have the convolution form of §4.3 and are evaluated for
all $\mathbf{q}$ at once by lattice FFT. $C_q$ ($n_\mu \times n_\mu$, Hermitian) is
factorized once per $\mathbf{q}$: Cholesky in the charge channel, positive
semidefinite up to numerical rank deficiency (single-device runs add a small
trace-proportional ridge; the distributed 2D-blocked factorization consumes $C_q$
directly), LU for the indefinite bispinor transverse channels (§8.2). Fits run on
the IBZ q set only (§5.4).

The Coulomb matrix every later chapter consumes is then

$$
V_{q,\mu\nu} \;=\; \sum_\mathbf{G} \tilde z^*_{q,\mu}(\mathbf{G})\;
\tilde z_{q,\nu}(\mathbf{G}),
\qquad
\tilde z_{q,\mu}(\mathbf{G}) = \sqrt{v_q(\mathbf{G})}\;
\mathrm{FFT}\big[e^{-i\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{r}}\zeta_{q,\mu}(\mathbf{r})\big],
$$

with $v_q(\mathbf{G})$ and its $\mathbf{q}\to0$ handling in Chapter 6. Production
runs stream the fit over band, grid, and q chunks under the memory planner, with ζ
on disk between stages (§12.2); no equation changes.
