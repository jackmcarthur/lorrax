# 5.5 Choosing the rank

$n_\mu$ is the basis-size dial of the calculation, the analogue of a plane-wave
cutoff. Convergence sets in near 8–12 points per band in the fitting window; cost
grows as $n_\mu^2$ (matrices) to $n_\mu^3$ (factorizations), so sweep rather than
defend. Account for the orbit-closure surplus (§5.4) when comparing counts, and
sweep $n_\mu$ together with the band window (`nval`, `ncond`, `nband`): enlarging
the window dilutes a fixed basis.

The fit's figure of merit is the per-$\mathbf{q}$ relative residual
$\|\rho - \rho_{\text{fit}}\|_F / \|\rho\|_F$ of §5.3's normal equations.

Disagreement with a reference that plateaus while $n_\mu$ increases is not a basis
problem; check conventions and cutoffs (App. E, starting with
`bare_coulomb_cutoff`). Bispinor calculations fit four densities with independent
ranks; the recommended charge:transverse ratio is ≈3:1 (§8.2).

> **Centroid window invariant.** The centroid selection window must span the
> sigma window $[0,\, \mathrm{nelec}+\mathrm{ncond})$ that the GW run consumes.
> `kmeans_cli` is decoupled from the deck: it never reads it, resolving its
> window from the WFN itself (`n_val = wfn.nelec`,
> `n_cond = wfn.nbands − n_val`) or the `--prune-n-*` flags, and that default is
> a superset of any deck's `ncond` — a shortfall means the window was narrowed
> explicitly. The pivoted-Cholesky rank gate (`LORRAX_CENTROID_RANK_TOL`,
> default 0.01) refuses when the certified rank falls short of the requested
> orbits; lowering the tolerance is a deliberate override. Basis adequacy in the
> wide-window regime: `docs/dev/isdf_basis_adequacy_at_large_nband.md`.
