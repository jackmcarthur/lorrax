# 12.3 Distributed linear algebra backends

Three input keys select who factorizes what: `distributed_cholesky` and
`distributed_lu` (`auto | off | cusolvermp | slate`; LU adds ScaLAPACK on the CPU
backend) cover the fitting-matrix factorizations and large solves;
`screening_solver` (`jax_native | cublasmp_ffi`) covers the Dyson solve (§7.3).
`auto` uses the best available library and falls back to the pure-JAX paths;
explicit requests fail loudly when unavailable (§2.1).

The vendor libraries have geometry limits, which LORRAX rejects with explanatory
errors instead of inheriting as hangs: cuSOLVERMp Cholesky and cuBLASMp require
square process meshes; cuSOLVERMp eigendecomposition deadlocks on non-square
meshes; SLATE rejects 1×q grids; manual `block_size` overrides on multi-rank
meshes are validated rather than trusted. Every backend × mesh × dtype cell is
exercised by the FFI contract suite (§2.3), including bit-determinism, and results
agree with the pure paths at 1e-14-class residuals. Practical guidance: leave the
keys on `auto`; choose SLATE explicitly on CPU nodes or non-NVIDIA hardware; keep
process meshes square when the distributed factorizations dominate.
