## Matched run and cost

All arms used the same `mpa_sigma.in` physics deck, fixed-N occupation hash
`0c4f1e84945d8b18`, P=4 (2x2), BFC@0.85, and source
`2e11b4133fe68961eff0a4bbebee7410a609c53d` (requested base merged with
`origin/feat/loewner-conditioning-2026-08-31`).  The fit work increased from
493 to 580 to 725 block dispatches for 8/10/12 poles.  The corresponding fit
kernel walls were 37.997, 57.282, and 96.497 s; complete read+fit+write walls
were 67.827, 95.105, and 152.688 s.  The fresh screening-stage walls that made
the 10/12 artifacts were 202.0 and 354.2 s.

The logical window count did not grow: all three geometries had the same six
named windows.  Pole count instead widened the negative-conduction pole-tail
support: scale span 132.504 (8), 269.235 (10), and 270.035 (12).  Eight poles
finalized 74 pairs and ran 105 tau dispatches in nine sweeps.  Ten and twelve
both stopped before pair selection, so they have no honest pair count or Sigma
wall.  Retrying the historical eight-pole plan cache did not force an
apples-to-oranges execution: its fingerprint missed on the 10-pole measure,
the cache validator replanned, and the same 1.41902e-3 refusal recurred.

The supplied 8-pole reference is reproduced from the historical 115-pair arm:
0.195876 meV max / 0.00883392 meV RMS and 63.179 s Sigma wall.  On the requested
current base, a cold replan selected only 74 pairs and moved those errors to
0.235789 / 0.00957467 meV.  Thus even the eight-pole plan changed; the 10/12
failure is not being hidden behind the older reference.
